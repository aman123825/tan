## Taking part in HearBloom (research software)

**What this is.** HearBloom is research software for practising and
exploring listening skills. It is **not a medical device** and it does not
diagnose hearing loss, auditory processing disorder, tinnitus or any other
condition.

**What is recorded.** When you complete an exercise the app stores, on this
device: your answers, response times, the exercise settings, optional
self-ratings (fatigue, confidence) and optional session names. If remote
monitoring is linked, the same records are uploaded to the study server.

**What is NOT recorded.** No audio from your microphone is captured. No
advertising identifiers are used. Nothing is shared with third parties.

**Your choices.**
- You can use the app without an account; data then stays on this device
  unless you link a clinician/study server.
- You can export everything (`Export my data`) at any time.
- You can delete everything (`Delete all my data`) at any time — locally and,
  if linked, on the server.

**Sound safety.** The app never changes your device's volume, caps its own
output levels, and asks you to set a comfortable level before listening
tasks. Stop any exercise that is uncomfortable.

**Agreement.** By continuing you confirm you understand the above, that
results are research measurements on uncalibrated consumer audio, and that
any hearing concern belongs with a qualified audiologist.
